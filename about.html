About Us

ようこそ (Welcome)

We (Royal) are a small consulting firm that has been in the industry since the 90s. Throughout the decades, we have had different names and different specialties. In the 90s we were a web design company, in the 00s we were a security consulting firm, and in the 10s we were an IT consulting company. We have worn different hats throughout the decades, but they have always been technical, and cutting edge. Today, we mostly do VR consulting, with some IT consulting on the side.

"Any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic." -- Arthur C. Clarke. We really enjoy that quote, and try to live it in our designs. Whether it is playing with fully immersive holography, or trying to prototype mid-air haptics, we strive to push the limits of what people believe is possible, and show them true magic.

We are Combobulate Tech, and we are here to combobulate the discombobulated, possibly before you even know you are discombobulated.

If you would like to learn more, reach out to us at info@combobulate.tech

We hope that you enjoy your stay, learn something cool, and let your imagination run wild with possibilities.
